## Inline

The Pythagorean theorem is $`a^2 + b^2 = c^2`$.

## Blocks

The divergence of vector $`\vec{\mathbf{F}} = [F_x, F_y, F_z]^\text{T}`$ is:

```math
\nabla \cdot \vec{\mathbf{F}} = \frac{\partial{F_x}}{\partial{x}}
                              + \frac{\partial{F_y}}{\partial{y}}
                              + \frac{\partial{F_z}}{\partial{z}}
```
